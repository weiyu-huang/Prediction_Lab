# Prediction Lab

## Vision

Prediction Lab is a long-term quantitative research laboratory focused on understanding probabilistic markets.

The project begins with European soccer because it offers rich data, mature markets, and a well-defined environment for research. Soccer is only the first laboratory.

Over time, the same research framework should extend to prediction markets such as Kalshi, Polymarket, and other domains where prices represent beliefs about uncertain future events.

The long-term ambition is to build an institutional-quality research platform capable of supporting an independent quantitative hedge fund.

---

## Principles

### Understand before optimizing.

Research begins with understanding how a system works, not with building increasingly complex models.

### Curiosity over profit.

The goal of each experiment is to learn something true about the market. Profit is a long-term consequence of better understanding, not the objective of individual experiments.

### Build assets that compound.

Data pipelines, experiments, infrastructure, and accumulated knowledge are long-term assets. Every iteration should make future research easier.

### Small discoveries compound.

Every week should answer one concrete question. Every month should unlock one new capability. Small improvements accumulate into lasting advantages.

### Reproducibility matters.

Every dataset should be rebuildable. Every experiment should be reproducible. Every conclusion should be supported by evidence.

---

## Research Workflow

Question → Hypothesis → Dataset → Experiment → Evidence → Understanding → Next Question

The output of research is not code.

The output of research is better understanding and better questions.

---

## Philosophy

Prediction Lab is designed to be played like a deep strategy game.

The reward is not placing a bet or shipping a model.

The reward is discovering how the system works, uncovering hidden mechanisms, and gradually building a more accurate mental model of the market.

Build slowly.

Understand deeply.

Compound forever.
