# Report Style Guide

Each Prediction Lab report answers one concrete research question. Reports prioritize quantitative evidence over plots and end with the next research question, forming a continuous research log.

## Standard Structure

1. Research Question
2. Executive Summary
3. Research Scorecard
4. Method
5. Results
6. Discussion
7. Next Experiment

## Executive Summary

Answer the main question in two to five bullets. State the strongest result, statistical significance, stability, and whether the hypothesis is worth pursuing.

## Research Scorecard

Summarize the most important findings in a small Metric / Value table that can be understood at a glance.

## Results

Prioritize quantitative tables in this order: Overall, By Season, By League. Keep the report concise; export large tables to CSV and show only the observations needed to answer the question. Display ROI together with its 95% confidence interval.

Include only metrics that add independent information. Profit is omitted because ROI and number of bets determine it.

## Discussion

Interpret the evidence concisely. Distinguish point estimates from statistically credible and stable results.

## Next Experiment

End with:

- **Question:** What remains unknown?
- **Experiment:** What is the simplest experiment that answers it?

Every report should make the next experiment obvious.
